## 📐 Classical Physics & Mechanics

### Einstein's Mass-Energy Equivalence
The energy $E$ of a physical system is directly proportional to its mass $m$:
$$E = mc^2$$

### Newton's Law of Universal Gravitation
The gravitational force $F$ between two point masses $m_1$ and $m_2$ separated by a distance $r$ is:
$$F = G \frac{m_1 m_2}{r^2}$$

## 📈 Calculus & Linear Algebra

### The Fundamental Theorem of Calculus
This formula computes the net change or the area under a continuous curve:
$$\int_{a}^{b} f(x) \, dx = F(b) - F(a)$$

### Matrix Multiplication Example
A $2 \times 2$ matrix operation layout:
$$\begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} x \\ y \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} ax + by \\ cx + dy \end{pmatrix}$$

## 📊 Statistics & Probability

### Standard Normal Distribution (Gaussian)
The probability density function for a standard normal distribution is expressed as:
$$f(x) = \frac{1}{\sigma \sqrt{2\pi}} e^{-\frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{x-\mu}{\sigma}\right)^2}$$
